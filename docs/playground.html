rioterm predictive-echo playground

A self-contained rioterm terminal with a simulated PTY: your keystrokes are echoed back after the latency below, exactly like a remote shell. With predictive echo on, a typed character appears immediately (marked tentative), then firms up when the simulated echo lands. Type at the $ prompt. Raise the latency to see predictions clearly; drop it near zero and they stay invisible (self-gating).
